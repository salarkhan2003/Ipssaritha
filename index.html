About DCP Saritha

A journey of commitment, administrative excellence, and dedicated service to the nation.

Service, Leadership & Community

DCP Saritha, IPS, belongs to the elite Indian Police Service. Recognized for her strategic oversight, operational precision, and human-centric policing, she has successfully helmed multiple sensitive postings. With an educational foundation from premium institutions, she cracked the Civil Services Examination with distinction.

Her core policing philosophies emphasize proactive community outreach, integrating technology for women safety via "SHE Teams", combatting substance abuse among youth, and bringing police administration closer to the general public.
