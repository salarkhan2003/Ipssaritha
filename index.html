Career Journey

2010 – 2020

Field Leadership

DySP field postings: Bodhan, Narsampet, Eluru, and Guntur West. Resolved crises, managed police divisions, and built trust across diverse communities.

2020 – 2024

CID & Women Safety

Directed the Women Protection Cell at CID. Led statewide child welfare campaigns and organized cyber safety and anti-human trafficking operations.

2025 – Present

DCP Administration

Selected into IPS cadre. Driving staff welfare programs, citizen outreach services, and administrative reforms for the Vijayawada Police Commissionerate.

Operation Swechha

A campaign focused on child protection during tenure at CID:
0+ Rescues
rescuing child laborers and reintegrating them into education.

Academic Excellence

Leveraging specialized learning (Psychology topper and LL.M Gold Medallist) to design victim care services and structure administrative decisions legally.

Core Philosophy

Methodical discipline, community accessibility, victim empathy, and total administrative transparency guiding daily official safety assignments.
